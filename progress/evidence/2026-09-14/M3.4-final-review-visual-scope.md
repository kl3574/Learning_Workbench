## Policy facts and causal limits

The Workbench ETag is `workspace digest + Workbench revision + full/active independent attempt identity` (`application/workbench.py:26–29`). Independent reads redact selections without writing the stored snapshot. Leaving an independent attempt can change the projection token at the same numeric revision. A stale independent token cannot authorize a late full-projection write; the same-revision rejection is explicitly covered by the existing `test_late_redacted_put_cannot_clear_selection_after_policy_changes` source. No tests were rerun here.

The test genuinely creates an independent attempt and submits it before its author review. That sequence can cross this projection boundary. The later author role change broadcasts frontend access invalidation, but role by itself is not an input to the backend Workbench ETag. Therefore “author switch changed the ETag” would be an unsupported claim. A pending candidate at reload with an old/missing token, or another actual save timing, remains a candidate explanation until a request/storage ledger distinguishes them. The screenshot does not prove an HTTP 412 occurred at capture, lost data, a newly introduced regression, or that the protection was unnecessary. Conversely, this inspection does not prove that every conflict-trigger path is correct or that the displayed conflict was subsequently resolved.
