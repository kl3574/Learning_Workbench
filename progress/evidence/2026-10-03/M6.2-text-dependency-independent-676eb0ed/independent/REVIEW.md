## Limits retained

The witness freezes what the Content owner can verify at candidate creation. **Arbitrary Content pin tampering before that freeze is not covered by this repair**: the witness is not an external immutable proof of an earlier unknown state. An otherwise valid concept pin already rewritten before creation may become the then-observed frozen fact. After-freeze pin divergence is checked against the recorded witness. This patch neither retroactively invents missing witnesses for dependency-bearing v1 records nor claims protection against a privileged attacker consistently rewriting all source records and hashes.
